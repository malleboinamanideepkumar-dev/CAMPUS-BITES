ABOUT US

The Story Behind Campus Bites

Campus Bites was created with one dream— to provide college students with quality food that is delicious, hygienic and affordable.

The founders experienced the struggle of finding good food during their college days. That experience inspired Campus Bites. Today we aim to serve thousands of students with the same passion and quality.
